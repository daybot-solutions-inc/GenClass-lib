Roadmap

Precise today. Catching more every round.

GenClass is tuned to almost never be wrong when it acts. At the default setting guard acts on about 8% of the cases where acting would help; the eager setting reaches about 1 in 5 on real apps. Each training round so far has lifted recall at the same safety level.
