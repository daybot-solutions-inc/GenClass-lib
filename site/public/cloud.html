What you get

Reliability you can show your boss.

Intervention analytics

Anomalies, interventions and undo rates per app, route and category, so you can prove what GenClass did.

Release comparisons

Every deploy compared with the last: stale overwrites, duplicates and failure storms per 10k sessions.

Alerts

Slack, email or webhook when a failure storm starts, a release regresses or undo rates climb.

Safe rollouts

Turn on guard mode for 5% of sessions, compare against observe, then promote. Kill it remotely in one click.

Shared policies

Modes per route, protected endpoints and aggressiveness, managed in one place instead of in every repo.

Team controls

Roles for engineers, managers and on-call, with a shared view of every production app.

Architecture

The cloud never sits in the hot path.

Inference stays in your users' browsers. The cloud connection only carries opted-in aggregate metrics and policies, and the runtime keeps protecting your app if it is unreachable.
